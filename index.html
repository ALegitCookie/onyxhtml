About Us:

Mission: Onyx FM Radio’s mission is to inform and empower the black community of Greater Orlando through music and community partnerships.

Vision: Our vision is to be Greater Orlando’s leader in radio/media for the black community.

Goal: Our goal is to ensure Greater Orlando’s black community is informed and empowered. Onyx FM Radio aims to be the bridge between citizens, community leaders and community resources.
